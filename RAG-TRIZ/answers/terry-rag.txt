1. **Data curation**: Ensuring the dataset is diverse, inclusive, and representative of the population it aims to serve.
2. **Regular auditing**: Periodically reviewing and updating algorithms to identify and mitigate bias.
3. **Transparency and explainability**: Providing clear explanations of how decisions are made and allowing for open discussion about potential biases.
4. **Human oversight**: Implementing mechanisms for human review and intervention to prevent harm or perpetuation of biases.
